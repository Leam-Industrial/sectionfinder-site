Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

SectionFinder collects very little: anonymous usage analytics and any email you send us.

1. WHO WE ARE

This policy explains how SectionFinder, operated by an individual in South Africa ("we", "us"), handles personal information in the SectionFinder app and at sectionfinder.com (together, "the App"). We are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Our Information Officer is the owner of SectionFinder, reachable at support [at] sectionfinder [dot] com.

The App is a free reference tool for structural steel sections. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT

2.1 Collected automatically
- Usage events through Google Analytics for Firebase: screens viewed, features used (for example a search, a calculator or a copy button), session length and app version.
- Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, language and an app instance identifier that is not linked to your name, plus app performance measurements (such as load times) from Firebase Performance Monitoring.
- Approximate location at country level, which Google derives from your IP address. We never ask for GPS or precise location.
- We do not run our own crash-reporting service. If you have chosen to share diagnostic data with Google, Google Play may show us anonymous crash statistics for the App.

2.2 Sent by you
- The content of any message you send to support [at] sectionfinder [dot] com, including your email address and anything you choose to write.

2.3 Stored on your device only
- Your selected country, your acceptance of the Engineering Disclaimer and your display preferences.
- Values you type into the Weight Calculator and Plate Girder tools. These are calculated on your device and are not sent to us.

2.4 What we do not collect
Your name, identity or passport number, payment details, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, precise location, or any special personal information as defined in POPIA.

3. WHY WE USE IT, AND OUR LAWFUL BASIS

- Understand which features are used so we can improve them: our legitimate interest (POPIA section 11(1)(f)).
- Find and fix errors and performance problems: our legitimate interest.
- Answer your email: your consent, given by contacting us.
- Comply with the law: legal obligation.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, build advertising profiles or make automated decisions about you.

4. ANALYTICS AND COOKIES

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase. Google acts as our operator and processes the data on our behalf under Google's terms. Event data is kept for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

The mobile app does not use cookies. The web version at app.sectionfinder.com uses Google Analytics cookies (such as _ga) to tell visits apart, and stores your preferences in your browser's local storage. You can block or delete these in your browser settings, and the web version still works without them.

Opting out. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings, and you can uninstall the App to stop all collection. On the web you can block cookies or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can also email us and we will help.

5. WHO RECEIVES YOUR INFORMATION

- Google (Firebase, Google Analytics, Google Play): analytics, performance monitoring, app distribution. United States and other Google regions. policies.google.com/privacy
- Supabase: hosts the section database. It receives your IP address and the queries the App makes, not your name. European Union (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). supabase.com/privacy
- GitHub Pages: serves sectionfinder.com. United States.

Some of these providers are outside South Africa. POPIA section 72 allows this where the recipient is bound by law, agreement or rules that give protection substantially like POPIA, and we rely on the providers' standard data-protection terms for that. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

6. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

- Analytics events: 2 months for event data and 14 months for user-level data, then deleted automatically.
- Google Play crash statistics: held by Google under its own retention rules.
- Emails to support: up to 24 months after the conversation ends.
- Settings on your device: until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

When a period ends, the data is deleted or anonymised.

7. HOW WE PROTECT IT

Data moves between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (TLS). Access to our systems and accounts is restricted to the owner. The section database is protected by Row Level Security and is read-only to the public. No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

If a breach puts your personal information at risk, we will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected as POPIA requires.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

You may ask us to:
- tell you whether we hold personal information about you and give you a copy;
- correct or delete it;
- stop processing it or withdraw consent you gave.

Because the App has no accounts, we can only find data linked to the email address you used to contact us. Analytics data is tied to a random app instance identifier, not to you, so we usually cannot match it to a person. Email support [at] sectionfinder [dot] com with the subject "Privacy Rights Request". We aim to reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

9. CHILDREN

The App is meant for engineering and construction professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. USERS IN THE UNITED KINGDOM

When we offer UK section data, UK law applies to UK users. Our lawful bases are legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) for fixing errors and keeping the App secure, and consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for analytics and for support emails. Analytics cookies and similar identifiers will be set only after you agree in a consent banner, and you can withdraw consent at any time. You may also ask for portability and restriction of processing, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at www.ico.org.uk. Transfers of UK data outside the UK rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision.

12. USERS IN THE UNITED STATES

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, so California residents have nothing to opt out of. California residents may ask us what we hold, request deletion or correction, and will not be treated differently for doing so. The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

13. THIRD-PARTY SITES

Links to Google, Supabase, GitHub and other sites are outside our control. Read their own privacy policies before you give them information.

14. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We will update the date at the top whenever this policy changes. For a material change we will show a notice in the App or on sectionfinder.com. Continuing to use the App after that means you accept the updated policy, but your rights under the law do not change.
